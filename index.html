Compare what the eye sees

Two stones can share a carat weight and face up at different sizes. Facet makes the millimeter estimate visible so weight does not masquerade as size.

Carry one clean brief

Copy the shape, weight, estimated dimensions, and verification checks into a note. Use the same brief when you compare listings or ask a jeweler a question.

Keep the seller out of the check

Verify the report number on the grading lab's own site. Then match the girdle inscription and exact measurements to the stone itself.

Put it inside a useful article

Publishers can use the one-tag web component, sandboxed iframe, or scriptless Link Mint. Each placement gets a distinct public attribution slug.

Put the stone on your own hand.

Stone on Hand calibrates a private hand photo with a credit card's known 85.60 mm long edge, then places the selected diamond's approximate true-size footprint on the ring finger.

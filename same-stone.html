Your comparisons

Exact means exact

The same grading lab and normalized report number is an exact certificate match. That is the strongest signal that two listings point to one physical stone.

Measurements are a warning

Shape, carat, length, width, and depth can flag a possible match when a report number is missing. It is a prompt to investigate, not proof by itself.

Nothing becomes a lead silently

Seller, URL, price, report number, notes, and captured text stay in your browser. A Stienhardt visit carries only shape, carat, and whether Same Stone found a match.

Same Stone is an open-source education tool made by Stienhardt & Stones, a New York City jeweler that sources certified Lab Grown Diamonds and hand-sets and finishes rings in NYC. Matching measurements are not proof of identity. Verify the grading report and girdle inscription.
